Frequently Asked Questions

Is this template free to use?

Yes — AdminLTE is open source under the MIT license. You can use it in personal and commercial projects.

Does it support dark mode?

Absolutely. Use the theme toggle in the navbar to switch between light, dark, and auto (system) modes.

How do I search the dashboard?

Press Cmd/Ctrl + K anywhere to open the command palette and jump to any page.

Can I use it for right-to-left languages?

Yes, toggle the direction button in the navbar to flip the entire layout to RTL.

Where are the charts coming from?

Charts use ApexCharts and the map uses jsVectorMap — both fully open source.
